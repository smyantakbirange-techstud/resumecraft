A FEW SMART TIPS

Small details.
Big difference.

A good resume is clear, relevant, and honest. Keep the reader's time in mind.

01
Keep it relevant

Highlight skills and projects that match the role you're applying for.

02
Show what you did

Use clear action words and describe your contribution to each project.

03
Make it easy to scan

Use consistent headings, readable text, and a simple layout.

04
Check before sending

Verify contact details, spelling, dates, and links before you apply.
